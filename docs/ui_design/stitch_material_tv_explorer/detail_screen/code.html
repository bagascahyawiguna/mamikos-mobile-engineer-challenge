The Spire Chronicle

Sci-Fi 2024 TV-MA

Summary

In the distant future, humanity has consolidated its remaining population within a single, towering megastructure known as The Spire. As resources dwindle and factions vie for control of the upper echelons, a young engineer from the lower levels discovers a secret that could either save the city or plunge it into total ruin.

Navigating political intrigue, dangerous alliances, and the harsh realities of their contained environment, she must decide who to trust in a world where everyone is fighting for a glimpse of the sky.
